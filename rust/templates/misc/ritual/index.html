Commence the Ritual!

Welcome to The Ritual! The Ritual is an initiation ritual for new members of Power Down. The new member sits down in VC with as large of an audience as can be gathered, and they proceed to go over as many characters as they can, guessing their names and powers.

We used to do this with someone pulling up every individual art piece in real time which, while endearing, became a bit of a pain once we crossed the 80 character threshold. So now the image and power reveal process is automated! Select your flavor of hellscape, and begin the ritual! (Call everyone to get on VC first <3 )
